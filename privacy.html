Privacy Policy

This page describes what data is collected when you visit dlunch.net or any of its subdomains, which third-party services are involved, and how you can opt out. Last updated May 2026.

Who Operates This Site

This site is a personal site operated by the author of dlunch.net. There is no company behind it and no commercial entity collecting data on its behalf. For contact information see the contact section on the home page.

Data Collected Directly

The site itself does not have user accounts, forms, comment systems, or any other mechanism that asks visitors to submit information. Nothing is collected directly by the site.

Standard web server access logs may record the IP address, requested URL, user agent, and timestamp of each request. These logs are used only for routine operational purposes (debugging, abuse prevention) and are not shared with third parties.

Third-Party Services

The site uses the following third-party services, each of which collects its own data subject to its own privacy policy.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics is used to understand aggregate traffic patterns: how many visitors arrive, which pages are read, which sources refer traffic. Google Analytics sets cookies (typically named _ga, _gid, and similar) to distinguish unique visitors across sessions, and may collect IP addresses, device information, and browser characteristics.

Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking site-wide by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Google AdSense

Some pages on this site may display advertising served through Google AdSense. AdSense and its advertising partners (including third-party vendors) may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this site and to other sites on the internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies by visiting aboutads.info. Some advertising may still be shown after opting out, but it will not be personalized.

Web Fonts

The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches these fonts, Google receives the request and may log the IP address, the requested resource, and standard request metadata as part of normal web traffic.

Cookies

The site itself does not set first-party cookies. The only cookies present in your browser as a result of visiting this site are those set by the third-party services described above (primarily Google Analytics, and Google AdSense where advertising is displayed).

You can block or delete cookies through your browser's settings at any time. Doing so will not affect the readability of the site, since the site has no functionality that depends on cookies.

Children's Privacy

The site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the third-party services used or in applicable regulations. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the email address listed on the contact section of the home page.
